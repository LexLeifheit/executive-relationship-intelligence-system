# Operating Playbook

## Weekly Review

1. Open the current report.
2. Handle overdue follow-ups first.
3. For every A-grade relationship, confirm that there is either a recent touch
   or a clear next action.
4. Add concise notes after each conversation.
5. Schedule follow-up dates while context is fresh.

## Scoring Guidance

Influence:

- `4`: Can directly shape a major opportunity, decision, introduction, or outcome.
- `3`: Can materially help through advice, credibility, access, or context.
- `2`: Useful relationship with situational relevance.
- `1`: Warm contact with limited strategic impact.

Health:

- `4`: Strong, active, and warm.
- `3`: Good but needs normal maintenance.
- `2`: Stale, uncertain, or underinvested.
- `1`: Damaged, distant, or dormant.

Grade:

- `A`: Requires active intentional maintenance.
- `B`: Worth periodic touch and context tracking.
- `C`: Keep lightly warm or archive from active review.

## Conversation Notes

Good notes are specific enough to make the next outreach feel human:

- Personal context.
- Recent life or work updates.
- Open loops.
- Ways you can help.
- A precise next reason to reconnect.
