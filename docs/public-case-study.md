# Public Case Study

## Executive Relationship Intelligence System

This is a small, private-first workflow I built to demonstrate how AI can help
with executive operations without replacing human judgment. The system turns a
structured relationship database into a weekly briefing that helps prioritize
follow-up, identify at-risk relationships, and maintain a strategic pipeline.

## Why This Matters

Policy and chief-of-staff roles often depend on invisible operating systems:
who needs context, which relationships need care, what commitments are open,
and where strategic attention should go next. This project translates that
kind of judgment into a lightweight workflow that can be run privately and
adapted for different leaders or teams.

## What The Workflow Demonstrates

- Designing a practical schema for executive relationship management.
- Using AI assistance to move from a Notion export to a documented, reusable
  workflow.
- Separating private data from public demonstration materials.
- Turning raw relationship records into a short weekly decision brief.
- Creating a reproducible process that can be reviewed, improved, and adapted.

## Public Demo Scope

The public repository includes:

- Sanitized sample data.
- A documented schema.
- A local report generator.
- A sample weekly brief.
- Operating guidance for adapting the workflow.

It does not include private relationship data, personal notes, generated private
reports, or raw exports.

## Weekly Brief Logic

The weekly report is designed for a Monday morning review:

1. `This Week`: relationships with follow-up dates within 14 days.
2. `At Risk Relationships`: high-influence relationships with lower health.
3. `FIELDWAVE Pipeline`: a role-specific pipeline sorted by last contact.

The same pattern could be adapted for stakeholder management, legislative
coalition tracking, grantmaker engagement, interagency coordination, hiring
pipelines, or board management.

## Relevance To Policy And Chief-Of-Staff Work

This project is intentionally modest. Its value is in the operating discipline:

- It keeps strategic relationships visible.
- It creates a repeatable weekly review habit.
- It distinguishes public artifacts from private context.
- It shows how AI can support judgment-heavy work through structure,
  summarization, and workflow design.
- It creates a documented system that another leader or team could inherit.
